# Dark Blue — Brand Research & Visual Identity

> Sources: the brand files in Google Drive (`Leaders Group/Dark Blue`) plus web research (Nov 2026).
> Colors below were **sampled from the logo file and the can photos**. Confirm them against an official
> guideline or Pantone list if Leaders or Meridyen can provide one.

## 1. Who is the brand
- **Product:** energy drink made in Turkey by **Meridyen Gıda**. The maker says it exports to 50+ countries.
- **Region:** sold across the Middle East (Lebanon, Syria, ...). In Syria it is listed as an imported cold drink.
  Distributed in Syria by **Leaders (Leaders of Grandness)**.
- **Sister brands from the same maker:** Just Power (energy), Just Coffee / Dark Cafe (cold coffee).
- **Sizes:** 250 ml slim can, 330 ml, 500 ml.
- **Range:** Original · Sugar Free (Şekersiz) · Multivitamin (12 vitamins) · **Mocktail line**: Berry, Mango, Mojito.
  The mocktail line is billed as a "Ready to Drink Energy Drink Mocktail", i.e. an alcohol-free cocktail.
  Packaging designer: Ceyhun Akgün, 2025.
- **Positioning:** young, dynamic, high energy. Usage occasions: study, work, sport, nightlife (Mojito).
  The maker's own trade copy: *"more than just an energy drink — a movement."*

## 4. Packaging system (every can)
1. **Body:** one solid flavor color covering about 75% of the can, matte finish.
2. **Seam lines:** thin white curved lines across the body, taken from a **basketball's seams**
   (designer's note: basketball texture + dynamic colors, aluminium base, matte finish).
   → We reuse this as the brand's background motif (`pattern: seams`).
3. **Name:** huge vertical condensed type (e.g. "DARK BLUE", "BERRY!") running up the can.
   Mocktail cans add a cocktail-shaker icon and fruit line-drawings.
4. **Bottom band:** silver aluminium, a thin red + white stripe, and the shark mark sitting on the stripe.

## 5. Tone & visual direction for billboards
- **Mood:** night, depth, deep ocean / electric blue light, predator energy. Confident and a little aggressive,
  never cute.
- **Hero:** the can is always the hero. Use it large, with a strong blue rim-light glow. One flavor per execution,
  and the background tints toward that flavor's color.
- **Key-visual prompts (AI):** deep dark-navy environments, underwater light rays, electric-blue rim light,
  splashes / ice / fruit for the mocktails, a sports arena (basketball) for Original/Sugar Free.
  Always add: *no text, no letters, no logos*. The can itself is composited from the real product file,
  never AI-generated.
- **Arabic copy:** short and punchy (≤ 6 words), Cairo Black. White on navy.

## 6. Motion language — preset `energetic`
| t (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | Background + seam lines **draw on** (basketball seams), slow push-in |
| 0.3 | **Shark "bite"**: logo punches in from 220% with rotation and overshoot |
| 0.7 | **Can slam**: product drops in with tilt and bounce, plus a 0.25 s white **impact flash** |
| 1.3 | Headline **whips in** word by word (skew + slide) |
| 2.2 / 2.8 | Subline fades in · CTA **punches** in |
| 3.4 | "Distributed by Leaders" fades in |
| → end | Can floats gently, background keeps pushing in. Last 0.4 s copy fades out (loop-friendly) |

Rules: snappy easing `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)`, short holds, no slow fades on the hero. For AI video (Higgsfield):
camera orbit / push-in on the can in deep blue water or electric light, 5–10 s, no text.
